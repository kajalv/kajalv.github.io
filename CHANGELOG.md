# Changelog

A running log of how this site has evolved, and the thinking behind each version.

---

## v4.1 — Personal touches

- Lime accent, IBM Plex Sans + Plex Mono, larger and higher-contrast type.
- Square corners instead of pills; plain text nav.
- Projects trimmed to nine; research moved above projects.
- Experience leads with reliability work; earlier roles and awards collapsed.
- Role highlights under each Intuit role.
- Education as rows, matching experience; stack shown as a config file.
- Certifications with auto-updating status.

---

## v4 — 2026 redesign

- Rebuilt from scratch: one HTML page, one CSS file, one small JS file. Bootstrap and jQuery removed.
- Status-page concept: "All systems operational" tag with a live San Diego clock, and a career uptime chart generated from data in `js/site.js`.
- Experience grouped by company; projects filterable by area.
- Light and dark themes; responsive layout; accessible markup.
- Compressed images, lazy loading, Open Graph tags.

---

## v3 — 2024 refresh

Content update after several years: new roles and awards, a new color scheme, and team name fixes.

## v2 — 2021 revamp

Reworked the About Me layout, education and hobbies pages, and font sizing across the site. Mobile CSS fixes.

## v1 — 2017

The original site, built during my first job: full-screen sections for education, skills, work, projects, awards, and hobbies, styled with Bootstrap and custom CSS. Updated over the next few years as I moved through grad school at Georgia Tech and into my role in San Diego.
